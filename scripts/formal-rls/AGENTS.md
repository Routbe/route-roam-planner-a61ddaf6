## RLS non-interference proof
- scripts/formal-rls-verifier.ts proves with Z3 that no rout_app policy admits another tenant's row; its reviewed exceptions live only in tenant-model.ts here, any new finding or unused exception fails src/test/rls-non-interference.test.ts, and a fixed finding leaves KNOWN_OPEN in the same change. Why: the list may only shrink, and a schema change cannot silently reopen cross-tenant access.
- Helpers whose meaning the encoder relies on (SRF_LEMMAS, PINNED_FUNCTIONS) are pinned to their exact SQL body; a changed body makes the dependent policy unproven. Why: the proof must never trust a function it has not read.
- A CONTRACT migration is released only after a CERTIFIED run of its certify script (pattern: certify-0277.ts, zero new or still-open findings, zero unproven, zero unused model entries) and a 0-deviation read-only pre-check on live data (tenant-backfill.ts --gateway); both are gates before the owner's GO, never a substitute. Why: a contract must be proven on the schema it produces and on the data it meets.
- Every role in policy-grant-audit.ts AUDIT_ROLES must hold EXECUTE on every function a policy it meets depends on (src/test/policy-function-grants.test.ts); a migration adding a policy helper grants it to those roles in the same file. Why: 0276 silently broke account merge with a missing grant.
